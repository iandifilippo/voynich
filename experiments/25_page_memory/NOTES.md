# 25 — Does the memory stop at the edge of the page?

## Why

Lines share vocabulary with the lines just before them (exp. 15, 22), and it isn't
positional (exp. 24). Three stories are left: the writer reuses what is on the page in
front of him; the writer has a habit that drifts as he goes; or the vocabulary follows the
subject. A page break separates them, above all in the herbal section, where every page is
a different plant.

## What I did

Lift = word types two lines share, over what the block's overall rates predict. Pairs of
lines 1-5 apart inside a paragraph, across a paragraph break, and across a page break (last
lines of one page, first lines of the next). Control with the same shape: the end of a page
against the start of a page 2-6 pages further on. Three blocks with one scribe and one
section each, so changes of hand or topic type can't cause anything: herbal A (hand 1, 95
pages), balneological B (hand 2, 19 pages), stars B (hand 3, 22 pages). Intervals from
resampling pages. Then a blunt version: from the last five lines of a page, can you pick
out the real next page among all the pages of the block?

## Result

| lift, lines 1-5 apart (all words) | herbal A | balneo B | stars B |
|---|---|---|---|
| inside a paragraph | 1.36 | 1.45 | 1.89 |
| across a page break | 1.36 | 0.92 | 1.32 |
| end of page vs start of a later page (control) | 1.12 | 0.86 | 0.96 |
| page break / control | 1.22 (p = 0.001) | 1.07 (n.s.) | 1.37 (p = 0.04) |

- **In the herbal, common words run straight through the page break.** The last lines of
  one plant page share as many word types with the first lines of the next plant page as
  two neighbouring lines of the same paragraph (ratio 1.00, interval 0.84-1.18). Holds when
  the leaf is turned (1.40) and from a verso to the next recto (1.31).
- The real next page is recognisable: among all herbal A pages it ranks in the top tenth
  26% of the time (chance 10%; mean percentile 0.64, p < 0.001).
- Stars B: the carry-over across pages is partly there (1.32; 0.70 of the within-paragraph
  level). Balneological B: gone (0.92, same as control), most clearly where the leaf is
  turned (0.68).

## Correction (after exp. 27)

My first write-up of this experiment went further: it said a herbal page has no vocabulary
of its own and concluded that vocabulary follows the order of writing and not the plant.
Exp. 27 ran the same measurements on a real text about plants (Pliny, book 16, by chapter)
and that conclusion doesn't survive:

- With all words counted, Pliny's chapters show no vocabulary of their own either. The
  all-words statistic is run by common words and can't see a subject. "No page vocabulary"
  on that statistic means nothing.
- With the 60 commonest words set aside, the herbal looks different: sharing drops by about
  a third at the page break (2.34 inside a paragraph, 1.70 across the break), and lines
  10-30 apart on the same page share more than chance (1.67, interval 1.15-2.34; control
  0.98). Pliny does the same at chapter breaks.
- The expected-sharing formula also counted word types that occur once, which can never be
  shared. Fixed; ratios are unchanged, absolute lifts moved by a few percent (more for the
  rarer words).

What stands: common words carry over the page break at full strength in herbal A, the next
page is recognisable, and the balneological quire breaks the pattern. What doesn't stand:
that the page has no vocabulary of its own. For the less common words it may well have.

## What this means

1. **The common-word layer is a habit of writing sequence.** It ignores the change of plant
   completely. That is the layer exp. 22 called the core.
2. **The herbal A pages are still mostly in the order they were written**, across leaf
   turns and from one leaf to the next.
3. **The balneological quire is different**: the continuity breaks at the leaf turn. Quire 13
   is the one that codicologists have long argued is bound in the wrong order. With 19
   pages I can't reorder it from this, but the measure sees the disturbance.
4. Whether the *rarer* words follow the subject is open, and exp. 27 is the place to look.

## Caveats

First lines of pages are paragraph openers and last lines are paragraph ends, which is why
the control uses the same shape. The balneological and stars blocks are small (17 and 20
page breaks); only the herbal result is firm.
